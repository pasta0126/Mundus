# donations Specification

## Purpose
Lets a visitor who wants to support Mundus financially find a way to do so.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy Me a Coffee link on the About page
The About page SHALL show a "Support this project" link or button that opens
the owner's Buy Me a Coffee page (`https://www.buymeacoffee.com/pasta0126`)
in a new tab.

#### Scenario: The support link opens Buy Me a Coffee
- **WHEN** a person uses the "Support this project" link or button on the About page
- **THEN** the owner's Buy Me a Coffee page opens in a new tab

### Requirement: Recognisable support button
The support link SHALL look like Buy Me a Coffee's own button - its yellow
colour and logo - and SHALL be centered in its section.

#### Scenario: The support button looks like Buy Me a Coffee's
- **WHEN** the About page is shown
- **THEN** the support link is yellow with the Buy Me a Coffee logo, centered

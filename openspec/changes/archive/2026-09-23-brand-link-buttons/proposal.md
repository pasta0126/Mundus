## Why

The Buy Me a Coffee, Discord and GitHub links are generic buttons. Styling them like each platform's own button makes them recognisable at a glance.

## What Changes

- The three external links use each platform's brand colours and logo, and are centered.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

(none)

### Modified Capabilities

- `donations`: the support link is styled as Buy Me a Coffee's own button.
- `feedback`: the Discord and repository links are styled as Discord's and GitHub's own buttons.

## Impact

- `frontend/src/about/BrandLink.tsx` (new), `frontend/src/about/AboutPage.tsx`.
